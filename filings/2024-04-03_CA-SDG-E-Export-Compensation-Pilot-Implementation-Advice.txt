Re: Response of the Vehicle-Grid Integration Council to Advice Letter 4407-E of
San Diego Gas & Electric Company.
Dear Sir or Madam:
Pursuant to the provisions of General Order 96-B, the Vehicle-Grid Integration Council
(“VGIC”) hereby submits this response to the above-referenced Advice Letter 4407-E of San Diego
Gas and Electric Company (“SDG&E”), Implementation of Dynamic Export Rate Pilot Program
Pursuant to Decision 23-11-006 (“Advice Letter”) submitted on March 14, 2024.
Decision (“D.”) 23-11-006, issued on November 15, 2023, directs SDG&E to conduct the
Dynamic Export Rate Pilot for customers on Schedules EV-HP, AL-TOU, and TOU-A.1 D.23-11-
006 also orders SDG&E to file a Tier 2 advice letter within 120 days to implement the pilot. VGIC
offers the below response to SDG&E’s Advice Letter.
I. SDG&E should begin designing and piloting no-regrets rate elements – including
those very likely to be required by upcoming guidance in R.22-07-005 – with EV-HP,
AL-TOU, and TOU-A customers who are interested in taking advantage of these
additional rate elements.
D.23-11-006 directs SDG&E to establish a dynamic import rate after guidance is issued in R.22-
07-005. Since the issuance of D.23-11-006, the Commission has also issued D.24-01-032, which
strongly indicates – and, given the scale of funding authorized in the Decision, all but finalizes –
which dynamic rate elements will be included in any upcoming guidance in R.22-07-005. D.24-
01-032 authorizes up to $36,695,000 to support the expansion of dynamic rate pilots in SCE and
PG&E service territory. These dynamic rate pilots apply to both imports and exports and include
marginal distribution pricing components. SDG&E’s export compensation pilot, as outlined in the
Advice Letter, addresses only a dynamic export component, and it also does not include any
