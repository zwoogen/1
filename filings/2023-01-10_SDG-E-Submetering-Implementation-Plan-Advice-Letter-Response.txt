Dear Sir or Madam:
Pursuant to the provisions of General Order 96-B and the extension granted by
Administrative Law Judge (“ALJ”) Brian Korpics in the November 15, 2022 email ruling, the
Vehicle Grid Integration Council (“VGIC”) hereby submits this response to the above-referenced
Advice Letter 4114-E of San Diego Gas & Electric Company (“SDG&E”), Establishment of the
Plug-In Electric Vehicle Submetering Protocol Implementation Plan Pursuant to Decision (D.) 22-
08-024 (“Advice Letter”) on January 10, 2023.
VGIC commends SDG&E’s efforts in developing a thorough implementation plan. VGIC
supports the Advice Letter; however, we provide additional context for SDG&E’s consideration as
it implements submetering. VGIC recommends that Energy Division approve the Advice Letter
without delay, as VGIC’s below recommendation can be addressed during implementation and in
subsequent efforts undertaken by SDG&E to support EV submetering in its territory.
I. SDG&E SHOULD ADD ELECTRIC SCHEDULE EV-TOU-5 TO ITS LIST OF
RULES AND RATE SCHEDULES REQUIRING REVISION UNDER D.22-08-024.
The Advice Letter proposes tariff changes to implement submetering within the following tariffs:
 Electric Rules 16, 19, and 45
 Electric Schedules EV-TOU, TOU-M, and EV-HP1
VGIC generally supports enabling submetering through revisions to the above-listed rules and rate
schedules. For residential customers, in addition to EV-TOU, VGIC also recommends SDG&E

permit customers using submetering to enroll in EV-TOU-5. Customers may wish to choose this
rate, which utilizes a basic service fee and lower TOU prices, over EV-TOU. However, without
submetering permitted, EV-TOU-5 customers would have to expose all of their load, including both
EV load and non-EV load, to the TOU price signals intended to shift EV charging. VGIC
recommends SDG&E enable submetering for EV-TOU-5, so customers can elect to participate in
this rate using only their approved EVSE submeter, rather than their home master meter.
II. CONCLUSION.
VGIC appreciates the opportunity to submit this response to the Advice Letter and looks
forward to collaborating with the Commission and stakeholders in this proceeding.
