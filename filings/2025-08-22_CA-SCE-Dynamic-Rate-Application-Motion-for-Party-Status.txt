Pursuant to Rule 1.4 of the California Public Utilities Commission’s (“Commission”)
Rules of Practice and Procedure, the Vehicle-Grid Integration Council (“VGIC”) respectfully
moves for party status in the above-referenced proceeding.
I. DESCRIPTION OF VGIC.
VGIC is a 501(c)6 member-based advocacy group committed to advancing the role of EVs
and vehicle-grid integration (“VGI”). through policy development, education, outreach, and
research. VGIC supports the transition to a decarbonized transportation and electric sector by
ensuring the value from flexible EV charging and discharging is recognized and compensated to
achieve a more reliable, affordable, and efficient electric grid. VGIC’s members include
automotive original equipment manufacturers (“OEMs”), EV service providers (“EVSPs”),
distributed energy resource (“DER”) aggregators, publicly-owned electric utilities, and
Community Choice Aggregators.
II. INTEREST IN THIS PROCEEDING.
In this consolidated application, Southern California Edison (“SCE”) proposes new
dynamic rates in compliance with the Commission’s Rulemaking 22-07-005 and California Energy
2

Commission’s (“CEC”) Load Management Standards. EV customers are interested in participating
in dynamic import and export rates and can shift load and exports in response to the dynamic price
signals SCE is proposing.
VGIC intends to participate in this proceeding to ensure that SCE’s proposed rate designs
send accurate and actionable price signals to EV customers. VGIC will offer factual contentions
related to how specific elements of SCE’s dynamic rates impact customer behavior, managed
charging adoption, and the ability of EVs to provide load flexibility and grid services. VGIC will
also provide legal and policy arguments regarding the alignment of SCE’s proposals with statutory
and regulatory objectives, including to maximize the use of VGI strategies and encourage the
deployment of EVs.1 These contentions are directly pertinent to the issues presented in the
application.
