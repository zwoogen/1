I. INTRODUCTION.
The Vehicle-Grid Integration Council (“VGIC”) respectfully provides its reply comments on the
Commission’s Order Issuing Notice of Proposed Rulemaking, filed February 13, 2026, proposing
the adoption of a new interconnection rule at 17.9.575 NMAC applicable to rural electric
cooperatives (“Proposed Rule”) and corresponding amendments to the existing interconnection
rule at 17.9.568 NMAC. VGIC timely submitted its initial comments on April 13, 2026.
II. INITIAL COMMENTS SHOW GENERAL CONSENSUS AMONG PARTIES
THAT THE BLANKET EXCLUSION OF BIDIRECTIONAL CHARGING
SYSTEMS FROM THE PROPOSED RULE SHOULD BE REMOVED.
In initial comments, the Renewable Energy Industries Association of New Mexico (“REIA-NM”),
Nuvve Holding Corp. (“Nuvve”), and – most notably – the New Mexico Rural Electric
Cooperative Association (“NMRECA”) emphasize that bidirectional charging systems should be
treated as other battery energy storage systems for interconnection purposes and, as a result, should
not be excluded from the Proposed Rule. VGIC commends NMRECA for its specific comment to
“delete the last sentence, ‘DER does not include vehicle-to-grid functionality[,]’ from the
definition of Distributed Energy Resource (DER)” and for recognizing the peak load reduction
potential of electric vehicles.
Given this broad consensus and NMRECA's recommendation, VGIC reiterates its initial
recommendation to remove the blanket prohibition on bidirectional charging systems from the
Proposed Rule. We also respectfully request that the utilities consider the supplemental guidance
we provided in our initial comments, whether by formally adopting it in the Proposed Rule or by
adopting it informally for internal use.
